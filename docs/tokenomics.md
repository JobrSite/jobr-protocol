# Tokenomics

*Status: draft framework. No figures below are final, and no coin address has been announced.*

Numbers are deliberately absent. A supply, split or schedule will be written here only after it is decided and stated publicly. Until then this page describes the questions the structure must answer.

## Roles a coin could play

| Role | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Payout unit | The medium in which assignments are funded and settled |
| Alignment | Gives long-term participants a stake in network quality |
| Access | Possible gating or weighting for heavy usage |

## Allocation categories under consideration

| Category | Intent |
|---|---|
| Network rewards | Fund payouts and early assignment volume |
| Liquidity | Support orderly trading |
| Builders | Reward people who build the bots and tools |
| Reserve | Cover long-term operations |

Percentages: **not stated**.

## Principles

- No hidden allocations. Every category is published with its terms.
- Schedules are public and verifiable onchain.
- Nothing here is final until marked stated in the [README](../README.md) register.
